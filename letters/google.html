My name is Gonzalo Enei. I’m a founder and product maker.

I co-founded Faces, a presentations platform where every slide is a software artifact. I was the CEO for 3 years. My co-founders run it today. We built several other AI-native products, including a Workspace where every tool was usable by humans and agents, and an extension that generated Interfaces using the underlying webpage as a backend. Hi Ventures and Bessemer backed us.

Before that, I was employee No.11 at Fintual, which makes investing easy for people in LatAm. I was Head of Growth and later VP of Product, as we grew from $10M to $650M in assets under management in four years. This was Sequoia’s first investment in Chilean founders, founders who in turn invested in Faces.

I believe in a future where humans function as “creative directors” of companies and operations are handled by agents. Where people are free from busy work and have room to let in the ingenuity and spark that is uniquely theirs. Building the systems that move us in that direction is tremendously exciting to me. It’s something I could do all day.

That’s what I’d like to build at Google, where a good idea reaches billions of people.
